# {{ title }}

Whether you are an AI builder, developer, business analyst, product professional, founder, student, aspiring AI builder, or simply curious about what others are creating, there is a place for you in the room.

You can come to pitch an idea, demo something you are working on, get feedback, meet potential collaborators, or simply listen and learn from what others are building.

Whether it’s an AI agent, automation tool, RAG system, prototype, early stage startup, business use case, or just an idea you are trying to validate, we want to hear about it.

**Bring it. Demo it. Listen. Learn. Connect.**

This meetup is a space for:

- Pitching and validating ideas
- Demoing prototypes and early stage projects
- Getting actionable feedback
- Learning from what others are building
- Finding collaborators, mentors, and potential users
- Exploring AI use cases from both technical and business perspectives
- Brainstorming product direction, architecture, scaling, and go to market ideas

You do **not** need to have a finished product, and you do **not** need to be technical to attend. An idea, a rough prototype, a business problem, or even curiosity is enough.

If you’d like to present, come prepared to share your **project or idea, the problem you’re solving, what you’ve tried so far, and the kind of feedback you’re looking for.**

[Improving](https://improving.com/) is hosting the event, with the meetup beginning at 5:30 PM.
